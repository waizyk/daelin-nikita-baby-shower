The Small Print

Privacy

This website was made for one family celebration: {{ EVENT.parents }}'s baby shower. It has no ads, no trackers and no analytics.

What we keep

  • Photos and videos you upload, plus the name and message you choose to add. Both are optional.
  • Your internet address, briefly, to stop misuse of the upload form. It's cleared within a day.

Where it goes

  • Uploads are stored for the family on this website (Cloudflare) and in the parents' own Google Photos album.
  • Nothing appears on the website until a host approves it. Hosts can hide or delete any upload.
  • We never sell or share anything with anyone else.

Google Photos

The hosts can connect the parents' Google account so uploads are saved to an album in their Google Photos. The site only asks to add to, and read back, the album it creates. It can't see the rest of their photos. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing something

Want a photo or video taken down? Ask any of the hosts ({{ EVENT.hosts }}) and they'll remove it.
