Google Photos album

Step 1 (admin, once). Paste the Google OAuth client from Google Cloud. The redirect URI to register is

Connect Google Photos Open the album in Google Photos
What happens when you tap Connect?
  1. Sign in with the parents' Google account (the one whose Google Photos should keep the album).
  2. Google will say “Google hasn't verified this app”. That's normal for a private family site: tap Advanced → Go to … (unsafe).
  3. Tick the boxes and tap Continue. The site can only add to, and read back, the album it creates. It can't see your other photos.
Sharing the album with family afterwards

Open the album in the Google Photos app → Share → Create link (or add people). Google doesn't let websites share albums for you any more, so this one step is done in the app.

Good to know

  • Everything guests send is kept, even if you hide it here. Once Google Photos is connected, new uploads go straight into the album, and the "Copy to Google Photos" button moves the ones kept on the website.
  • Until then, the website keeps them (up to about 900 MB; big photos are resized to 2560 px, and videos can be up to 150 MB each).
  • Hiding only removes it from this website's scrapbook. Hidden items still kept on the website can be deleted for good from the Hidden tab.
  • Photos sent to the Drive folder need adding to the album by hand.
  • The QR sign to print is at /sign.
